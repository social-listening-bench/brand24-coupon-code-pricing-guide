## Corporate Reputation & PR Crisis: Early Warning Mechanics

In modern digital ecosystems, corporate reputation destruction originates on fringe communities hours before surfacing in mainstream media:
1. **The Reddit Sentiment Blindspot**: Traditional press monitoring tools crawl legacy RSS news feeds, completely missing viral consumer complaints gaining traction on Reddit and TikTok. Brand24 indexes social threads in real time, alerting communications teams within minutes.
2. **Sarcasm & Dialect Misclassification**: Keyword-only tracking engines misclassify sarcastic complaints as positive sentiment. AI-driven sentiment analysis isolates contextual nuance, preventing executive teams from being blindsided by PR crises.
3. **Influencer Reach Prioritization**: When brand crises emerge, prioritizing response order based on author follower counts and engagement ratios prevents amplifying low-impact commentary while rapidly de-escalating high-leverage brand threats.
